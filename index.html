🏛️ LIC Official Update
LIC Jeevan Utsav — New Whole Life Plan Launched
LIC has launched Jeevan Utsav, a non-linked, non-participating individual savings whole life plan
Provides guaranteed additions of ₹40 per ₹1000 basic sum assured every year throughout premium payment term
Policy offers flexibility with multiple premium payment options — 5, 6, 10 or 15 years
Maturity benefit is payable as a lump sum and life cover continues for entire lifetime
Source: LIC India Official Website Read on LIC ↗
📢 Important Notice
LIC Premium Payment — Online & Offline Options Available
LIC policyholders can now pay premiums online through the official LIC portal, mobile app, net banking and UPI
BHIM UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe and all major payment apps are accepted for premium payment
Policyholders can also pay at any LIC branch, authorised collection centres and Common Service Centres
Premium payment receipts are sent instantly to registered email and mobile number
Source: LIC India Official Website Read on LIC ↗
💰 Bonus Update
LIC Declares Bonus for Participating Policies
LIC has declared bonus for all with-profit (participating) policies for the financial year
Simple reversionary bonus, final additional bonus and interim bonus are added to eligible policies
Policyholders are advised to check their policy status on LIC portal or contact their agent
Contact Mrs. Sudha Agarwal to know the exact bonus amount for your LIC policy
Source: LIC India Official Website Read on LIC ↗
🏥 New Plan
LIC Arogya Rakshak — Health Insurance Plan
LIC has introduced Arogya Rakshak, a health insurance plan offering hospital cash benefit
Daily Hospital Cash Benefit payable for each day of hospitalisation due to sickness or accident
Major Surgical Benefit covering 50+ listed surgical procedures at a fixed benefit amount
Available for individuals aged 18 to 65 years with simple documentation process
Source: LIC India Official Website Read on LIC ↗
⚠️ Advisory
LIC Warning — Beware of Fraudsters and Fake Agents
LIC warns policyholders to beware of fraudulent calls, messages and emails claiming to be from LIC
LIC never asks for your Aadhaar, bank details, OTP or password over phone or email
Always verify your agent's ID card and IRDA licence number before making any payment
For any doubt, call LIC toll-free number 1800-227-717 or visit nearest LIC branch office
